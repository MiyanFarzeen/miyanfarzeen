RESULT 01 / 0720 YEARS

Oman

Where I grew up. Where I learned to belong.

Muscat was home for my first 20 years.

I grew up among people from many countries and cultures, and all those differences became one community: in classrooms, on football pitches, and in friendships that outlasted school.

Growing up in Oman taught me that different cultures can still feel like home.

Beauty found

The Omani desert at sunset.

All day, it looks like nothing but sand. Then the sun goes down, and suddenly it’s beautiful.

Sometimes beauty isn’t about what’s there. It’s about how you look at it.

RESULT 02 / 0712 MONTHS OUT

The comeback

12 months away. One season that changed everything.

A serious football injury kept me off the pitch for almost a year. For a while, it looked like my playing days were over.

I stayed with the team from the sidelines, committed to rehab, and worked my way back, one day at a time.

That season, we won the CBSE Football Championship in Oman.

Beauty found

The comeback.

What looked like the end of my football story became one of its best chapters.

RESULT 03 / 07KOCHI

Off Thrift Store

My first business. My first lesson in seeing value differently.

Back in India for my B.Com, I co-founded Off Thrift Store, a pre-loved clothing business I ran for four years.

I learnt by doing: sourcing, pricing, selling, marketing, and figuring out why people choose one thing over another.

It was my first real taste of business and consumers, and it led me to an MBA.

Beauty found

A second life.

A shirt someone stopped wearing became someone else’s favourite. The product hadn’t changed. The perspective had.

RESULT 04 / 07AHMEDABAD

MICA

Where I learnt that showing up for people matters.

MICA was my first time living away from home, and my biggest lesson came from Placecomm, not a classroom.

On the Senior Placement Committee, I helped an entire batch through one of the biggest moments of business school: bringing in recruiters, supporting students and managing expectations through plenty of uncertainty.

Beauty found

The face after the offer comes through.

That moment when someone realises, “I got it.” Helping create it is something I’ll always be proud of.

RESULT 05 / 07OFF THE CLOCK

The things I’ll always say yes to

Football, food and travel.

Football

Manchester United on the screen, football on the pitch, a captain’s armband at school. Ask me “Football?” and it’s almost always a yes.

Food

It started with shawarma and Arabic food in Oman. Then came butter chicken, Kerala sadya and Lucknowi galouti kebabs. I’ve accepted I’ll never pick one favourite.

Travel

Rishikesh, Goa, Thailand, Malaysia and plenty of India in between. I love arriving somewhere new and figuring it out as I go.

RESULT 06 / 07PERSPECTIVE

What beauty means to me

I’ve found beauty in unexpected places.

  • A desert that looked empty until sunset.
  • An injury that felt like an ending until I came back.
  • A shirt no one wanted until it became someone’s favourite.
  • A nervous face until the offer came through.

Beauty isn’t fixed. It changes with the place, the moment and, most of all, the person looking.

For me, beauty is perspective.
